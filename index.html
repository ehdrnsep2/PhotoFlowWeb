Your photos, in the right place.

PhotoFlow turns your chosen Google Photos albums into an ambient Android TV screensaver and brings photos from selected device folders to a local lock screen.

Android TV Ambient ScreensaverLocal Lock Screen

One app, two photo experiences

SCREEN SAVER

Google Photos ambient playback

Connect your account, choose albums through the Google Photos settings flow, and enjoy a randomized slideshow when Android TV enters ambient mode.

LOCAL PHOTOS

Device folders for your screensaver

Add folders from the device and mix local photos into the screensaver. Folder access is separate from your Google Photos connection.

LOCK SCREEN

Random local lock-screen photos

Choose a separate local folder and let PhotoFlow change the lock-screen image when the device locks. Portrait-only selection is available.

PERSONALIZE

Make playback feel yours

Set timing, transitions, image sizing, background treatment, clock, date, and video behavior from the screensaver settings.

Privacy by design

PhotoFlow keeps Google Photos focused on the ambient slideshow experience. It does not provide an interactive Google Photos gallery or album-browsing grid.

  • Google Photos media is requested for active screensaver playback and held in memory only.
  • Local photos are read only from folders the user explicitly selects.
  • Google Photos media is not sold, advertised with, or shared for unrelated purposes.
  • Separate controls manage Google Photos albums, screensaver folders, and lock-screen folders.
  • The user can change album selections or disconnect Google Photos at any time.
