Your photos. Your TV.

PhotoFlow turns a Google Photos album selected by you into a beautiful randomized screensaver for Android TV.

Android TV Screensaver

How PhotoFlow works

1. Connect

Sign in with your Google Account and grant the permissions requested by PhotoFlow.

2. Choose an album

Select the Google Photos album you want to use as your TV screensaver source.

3. Sync locally

PhotoFlow synchronizes the selected media to the Android TV device's private local storage for screensaver playback.

4. Enjoy

Your selected photos are displayed as a randomized slideshow while the Android TV screensaver is active.

Privacy by design

PhotoFlow uses Google Photos data only to provide the screensaver experience requested by the user.

  • Photos are not sold.
  • Photos are not used for advertising.
  • Photos are not shared with third parties for unrelated purposes.
  • The user can disconnect Google Photos and remove locally synchronized data.
