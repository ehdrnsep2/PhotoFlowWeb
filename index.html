Your photos. Your TV.

PhotoFlow turns the Google Photos albums you choose into a beautiful, personalized screensaver for Android TV.

Android TV Screensaver

How PhotoFlow works

1. Connect

Sign in with your Google Account and grant the permissions requested by PhotoFlow.

2. Choose your albums

Select one or more Google Photos albums to use as your TV screensaver source.

3. Personalize

Choose the timing, transitions, image sizing, background treatment, clock, date, and video options.

4. Enjoy

Your selected photos are displayed as a randomized slideshow while the Android TV screensaver is active.

Privacy by design

PhotoFlow uses Google Photos data only to provide the screensaver experience requested by the user.

  • Photos are not sold.
  • Photos are not used for advertising.
  • Photos are not shared with third parties for unrelated purposes.
  • Media is requested for active playback and held in memory only.
  • The user can change album selections or disconnect Google Photos at any time.
